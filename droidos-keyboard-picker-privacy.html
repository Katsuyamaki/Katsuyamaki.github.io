Privacy Policy for DroidOS Keyboard Picker

Effective Date: August 31, 2026

1. Overview

DroidOS Keyboard Picker is a local Android utility from KatsuyaMaki LLC that helps users open Android's standard keyboard picker and, on supported Samsung cover screens, optionally use Shizuku elevated access to switch the active keyboard.

2. Information We Collect

DroidOS Keyboard Picker does not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal data. The app does not include advertising, analytics, telemetry, user accounts, or a remote backend service.

The app does not read or store the content you type, passwords, clipboard contents, files, photos, contacts, messages, precise location, or other personal content.

3. Shizuku Elevated Access

The optional Samsung cover-screen keyboard switching feature uses user-authorized Shizuku elevated access because the required cover-screen switching behavior is not available to a normal application through standard Android APIs alone.

Elevated access is used locally on the device only when needed to perform a user-requested keyboard switch. It is limited to temporary system-level input and keyboard-management operations required for that feature. These operations do not collect or transmit the content you type or other personal data.

Shizuku authorization is optional. Without Shizuku authorization, the app can still open Android's standard keyboard picker, but the Samsung cover-screen switching feature may be unavailable.

4. Network Access

The app does not request the Android INTERNET permission and does not send app data to KatsuyaMaki LLC or to an analytics or advertising service.

5. Third-Party Software

If you choose to use the optional elevated switching feature, Shizuku is a separate third-party application and service that you install, start, and authorize independently. Your use of Shizuku is subject to its own terms and privacy practices.

6. Data Retention and Sharing

Because DroidOS Keyboard Picker does not collect personal data, KatsuyaMaki LLC has no personal data from this app to retain or share.

7. Children's Privacy

DroidOS Keyboard Picker is a general-purpose utility and is not directed to children under 13. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the app's functionality or data practices change. The current version will remain available at this page with its effective date.

9. Contact
