DroidOS Portals — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 22, 2026 Developer: KatsuyaMaki LLC Public HTTPS URL: https://katsuyamaki.github.io/droidos-portals-privacy.html

This policy covers DroidOS Portals on supported Samsung Galaxy Z Flip devices. Features vary by app version, Samsung firmware, permissions, and purchases. Some Internal Testing versions do not include paid checkout; Google Play builds that offer lifetime Portal Mods use the Google Play Billing flow described below. A feature's availability never automatically grants an optional permission or consent.

Overview and local information

DroidOS Portals configures Samsung cover-screen Home and Recents portals, Ambient pages, ring navigation, and optional device controls. Manually tapped legacy portal widgets are usable without Accessibility.

The app keeps chosen destination apps and package/activity names, widget and Ambient layouts, background choices, MultiStar drawer/resize preferences, local Samsung recovery snapshots, visibility conditions, optional-feature consent, and settings in private on-device storage. It may inspect installed launchable apps and Samsung cover-screen state to provide these controls.

There is no Portals developer account, independent purchase-verification server, advertising SDK, or analytics/telemetry SDK in these builds. Portals does not send screen contents, local screenshot previews, notification text, selected apps, or portal settings to a KatsuyaMaki LLC server. It does not sell user information.

Accessibility and ring controls

After your separate affirmative Allow in the app and Android's own Accessibility Settings authorization, the AccessibilityService can inspect cover-screen windows. Depending on the feature, this can expose visible text, app/package names, view identifiers, window structure, and other sensitive content. Portals processes that information locally to recognize cover scenes, identify supported controls, navigate or perform user-directed taps and swipes, and run separately configured dwell/visibility rules.

Accepting Accessibility does not opt you into screenshot previews, Lock/AOD, cross-app Ambient pages, Notification Access, Shizuku, or a paid Mod. Deny in Setup stops Portals' Accessibility-driven processing without revoking Android's separate service grant. You can also disable the service in Android Settings. Manual portal taps remain available without Accessibility.

Optional screenshot previews

An individually enabled portal can use Accessibility's screenshot capability to capture its destination-app window after a launch. Previews may include messages, photos, account details or other sensitive content, and protected windows may reject capture. The most recent preview is stored privately in a no-backup local directory. Turning off that preview or deleting its portal removes the corresponding saved preview. Revoking in-app Accessibility consent stops further capture processing; existing explicitly saved previews can still be removed through their portal's settings or deletion. These images are not uploaded by Portals.

Optional Ambient Lock/AOD and cross-app overlays

Ambient widgets can optionally appear on the cover lock screen or Always On Display (AOD). Lock/AOD display is off by default. Third-party widgets can reveal private content to someone viewing the locked cover, including messages or calendar, health, or financial details. Portals does not redact widget content. Passive locked Ambient is intended to be view-only; disable it if it affects unlocking.

Where the Visibility Triggers+ Mod is available and verified as owned, each Ambient page has an independently consented Show over other apps switch, off by default. Following a separate in-app disclosure and affirmative agreement, a page may appear above an ordinary unlocked cover app when its selected app comes on screen, goes off screen, posts a qualifying new alert, or has no qualifying active alert (depending on the selected rule). App/foreground rules use Accessibility's cover app identity and window type; alert rules additionally require Android Notification Access to check notification package and status. The app does not need to read or save notification text for these rules.

These checks cannot guarantee recognition of every sensitive third-party screen; hosted widgets may cover another app's content and may receive taps. You can dismiss visible pages by the ring or switch the per-page option off. Paying for a Mod does not grant permissions or opt-ins. No Android Display over other apps special permission is requested for this feature; it uses the separately enabled Accessibility service.

Optional Notification Access and Samsung controls

Android Notification Access is broad and can expose other apps' notifications. When you separately grant it, supported Portal features use notification source/package identity, presence, clearable state, and, for opted-in media conditions, media playback state rather than saving notification message titles or bodies. Notification-specific functionality varies by device and version. The grant can be removed in Android Settings.

Separately installed and authorized Shizuku may allow explicit local changes to compatible Samsung MultiStar App Drawer, favorites, and Resize state, or a user-requested guarded app-task restart. A separately opted-in notification-source lookup may inspect a bounded part of the device system log and could encounter sensitive log content. Samsung state snapshots and support diagnostics remain in private app storage unless you deliberately copy/share a report. Portals does not upload them to its developer.

Optional Google Play Billing and purchase records

On Play-distributed builds offering lifetime Portal Mods, selecting Checkout, Restore, or Refresh interacts with the Google Play Billing service. Portals receives the relevant product identifiers, Google Play prices/offers and owned-purchase information, potentially including purchase state, quantity, signed purchase JSON, signature, purchase token and acknowledgment status. It uses this data to confirm ownership, unlock the purchased Mod, avoid duplicate purchases, acknowledge verified purchases, and detect refunds/revocations.

A fresh Play-owned query and locally verified receipt/signature are required to grant paid functionality. An authenticated, signed ownership snapshot (including purchase tokens) is saved in app-private no-backup storage; it is updated by later successful ownership checks and is not sent to a KatsuyaMaki LLC server. A cached entitlement may expire until Google Play can verify it again.

Google Play presents its own checkout/promo redemption and processes payment/account/transaction information according to Google's terms and privacy practices. Portals does not receive payment card numbers or billing addresses, run its own checkout backend, or transmit screenshots or notifications as part of billing. Google Play's services may communicate with Google servers to process transactions and ownership queries. Purchases are optional; ordinary free portals remain usable without them.

Backups, retention and deletion

App settings and ordinary preferences may be included in Android-managed backup when enabled on the device. Screenshot previews and signed Play purchase snapshots are stored in no-backup locations. Saved screenshots can be removed through the portal's preview settings or by deleting that portal. App-private settings and snapshots are normally removed when the app is uninstalled or its data is cleared, subject to Android backup/restore behavior. Portals does not operate a developer database or user account to delete. Separate Google Play purchases, Android/Samsung services, third-party widgets, browsers, and Shizuku are governed by their respective providers' privacy and retention practices.

Children's privacy

Portals is a general-purpose Samsung cover-screen utility, not intended for children under 13. It does not knowingly collect children's information for KatsuyaMaki LLC.
