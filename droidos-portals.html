DroidOS Portals

A Samsung Galaxy Z Flip cover-screen app launcher from KatsuyaMaki LLC. Features depend on installed version, Google Play entitlements, Samsung firmware, and optional separately authorized services. Public-release availability may differ from Internal Testing.

Features

  • Configurable Home Portals, Recents portals, and optional Ambient cover-screen pages, including opt-in lock/AOD widgets.
  • Flash-ring gestures for navigation and user-selected actions. Separately opted-in paid Visibility Triggers+ can show Ambient pages over eligible unlocked cover apps based on foreground or notification conditions.
  • Optional Samsung MultiStar drawer, favorites and resize controls using separately authorized Shizuku access.
  • Optional per-portal screenshot previews, kept in private local storage.
  • Manual portal use remains available without enabling Android Accessibility.

Permissions and user choice

Ring navigation uses separately granted Android Accessibility permission plus an explicit Allow/Deny choice inside the app. Screenshot previews, notification-specific functions and Shizuku access have independent controls. Sensitive data can be visible to Android's broad grants; see the policy for details.

Optional lifetime Portal Mods

Where offered in the installed Google Play version, Mod Shop uses native Google Play Billing for optional lifetime Mods, including Infinite Portals+, Visibility Triggers+, and Visual Customization+. Purchases are signed-verified locally and can be refreshed or restored through Google Play. Ordinary manual portals remain available for free; buying a Mod does not enable Accessibility, Notification Access or other optional consent.
